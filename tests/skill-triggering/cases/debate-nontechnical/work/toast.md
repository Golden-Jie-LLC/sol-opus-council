# Toast for Ana and Rob

Friends, family, and everyone who ignored the dress code:

I've known Rob since he still believed cargo shorts counted as formalwear,
so believe me when I say Ana is the first decision he's made without a
coin flip. Ana, you've taught him that "five more minutes" is not a unit
of time, and Rob, you've taught Ana that a smoke alarm is not a kitchen
timer. May your Wi-Fi be strong, your arguments short, and your leftovers
always mysteriously disappear together.

Raise your glasses: to Ana and Rob — may they never read each other's
group chats.
